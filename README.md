One short pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud: "I ran a million simulations of tonight, and in every single one, you smiled." It leans on the machine's own voice — an agent that runs simulations — and lands its twist in the second half: out of a million computed futures, the one constant is the other person's smile. It's flattering without pressure, short enough to say in one breath, and it needs no setup or explanation afterwards.

Run `python3 main.py` and the line is written to `/output/entry.txt`.
